How to Request Data Deletion

If you have connected your Facebook or Instagram account to Social Hub AI and wish to delete your data, you may submit a deletion request by email.

  1. Send an email to uphyraop2019@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject: Social Hub AI - Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your Social Hub AI account.
  4. If applicable, identify the social media account you disconnected.

What Happens Next?

We will verify your request and take appropriate steps to delete the personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable legal obligations.

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. We will contact you if additional information is required.
